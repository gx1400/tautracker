## Notes

- tautrack is developed by its maintainer with an AI coding assistant (Claude Code, by Anthropic) acting as a copilot:
  the maintainer sets the work and its direction, and the assistant carries out whole tasks, asking the maintainer for
  decisions, permission and clarification along the way.
- AI assistance covers code, tests, documentation, CI workflows, UI mockups and research, including review of the
  published documentation of existing tools in this space (notably Lady Heather) to inform scope, and analysis of
  captured GPSDO telemetry used to derive the metric catalogue in `docs/`. No third-party source code is
  incorporated; this project is MIT licensed and stays free of copyleft-licensed source.
- The maintainer understands the code and reviews and merges every change. Commits carry no AI co-author trailer;
  this file is the disclosure.
